## 3. CTS-14 — the second notional, and the rate that binds there

Twelve `MKT_RAW` fills at 23 shares, CHF ≈ 3,873–3,879 notional, across three runs
(`…T083909Z`, `…T084455Z`, `…T084635Z`).

**The rate binds, and it is measured at 5.0 bps — not the 6.0 in canon.**

| | value |
|---|---|
| implied bps, min / max | 5.0000000000 / 5.0000002578 |
| σ across 12 fills | 7.1e-08 |
| commission range | CHF 1.936600 … 1.939590 |
| what canon's 6.0 bps predicts | CHF 2.3239 … 2.3275 |
| schedule floor | CHF 1.50 — far below, so the floor is not what is charged |

⚑ **What discriminates a rate from a floor here is the variation, not the level.**
The measured 5.0 bps charges CHF 1.9387 at this notional against a
routing-weighted `min_per_order` of 1.9291 — **CHF 0.0096 apart**, which no single
fill could separate. But the charge **tracks the fill price proportionally**:

    commission max/min ratio = 1.001543943
    notional   max/min ratio = 1.001543943

Identical to nine decimal places, across **eight distinct commission values** on
eight distinct notionals. A floor would have been constant. **It is a rate.**
This is the check the lane required before Marcel's ruling could be written, and
it passes.
